# Soft-delete collection items as one recoverable action

An Instructor removes a Collection Round, Collection Group, or Collection Form through a single Collection Deletion: a soft delete that cascades to everything beneath the target, hides it from every Instructor and Student view, retains all records and files, and is recorded with the acting Instructor and time. Any Instructor may restore a deletion from the deletion log with a required reason; a restore returns exactly the items that one deletion removed, in their prior state, and only while every ancestor is live. Deleting items inside a closed Collection Round, or restoring them there, requires reopening the round first; the whole round may be deleted or restored while closed.

We rejected hard-deleting never-opened drafts because it erases form and membership audit history that Instructors acting on behalf of one another rely on, and would need an exception to the no-hard-delete rule for negligible storage savings. We rejected a separate archive state alongside deletion because two removal concepts with different visibility and eligibility rules add branches to every collection read path for one user need: removing items from sight. We rejected automatically reviving a deleted item when an Instructor creates one with the same name, because a matching name does not establish identity and revival would merge a previous intake's Students, submissions, and deadlines into a new one; structure is reused through form templates instead. We accept that Students lose sight of their own submissions in a deleted item until it is restored, that deleted rows remain in storage until a retention policy is agreed, and that every collection read path, background job, and index must exclude deleted items.
